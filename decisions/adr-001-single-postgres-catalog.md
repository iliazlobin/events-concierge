# ADR-001: Single-Postgres catalog with versioned-replay ingest and tenant-overlay serving

**Status:** accepted — with owner-ratification riders (see Consequences) · **Date:** 2026-07-10

## Context

The discovery layer is pulled apart by three forces that must be settled in one topology: freshness (NFR-1: a newly published event discoverable within ≤6h; interactive EventRequest p95 ≤5s from cache), quota (Ticketmaster hands out one shared app key at 5 rps / 5,000 calls/day with a size×page<1000 deep-paging cap — FR-3.4/AC-20 — which a naive per-request fan-out exhausts at roughly 4k users, hence the read-through catalog crawl mandated as launch architecture by FR-3.3/AC-21), and identity (canonical_event_id keys both the FR-8.1 durable workflowId and the FR-9.2 calendar idempotency hash, so a dedup mistake propagates into side-effect keys, while dedup thresholds are medium-confidence and will need post-launch recalibration). The corpus is small — ~150–500k active canonical rows, single-digit GB, ~12.5 QPS interactive peak at 100k users — three orders of magnitude inside pgvector's documented win regime for filter-heavy hybrid retrieval. Binding constraints:

- **FR-3.3/AC-21** — scheduled geo/category-sharded catalog crawl; a second tenant adds zero live TM calls.
- **FR-3.4/AC-20** — 5 rps, 5,000/day, never size×page≥1000; facet partitioning, not deep-paging.
- **FR-3.5/AC-24** — Meetup/Luma discovery only under the requesting user's own credential, never a shared token.
- **FR-3.6** — SerpApi google_events as cross-source top-of-funnel, deduped across providers before per-source enrichment; Eventbrite discovery arrives exclusively here (FR-3.2/AC-19).
- **FR-3.7/AC-22, FR-3.8/AC-17** — ACL normalization to schema.org/Event; dedup into CanonicalEvents before ranking, one stable id, all per-source registration URLs retained.
- **FR-1.3/FR-1.4/NFR-7 (AC-3)** — forced RLS, non-owner non-BYPASSRLS role, SET LOCAL tenant context, fail-closed to zero rows; zero cross-tenant leakage.
- **FR-8.7a/NFR-17 (AC-60)** — organizer cancel/reschedule detected ≤6h for every registered/scheduled event; TM modality = crawl delta plus re-poll.
- **FR-3.9/FR-10.1/AC-23** — automation_allowed/paid_allowed as per-source-per-modality data, effective without a deploy.
- **O-8** — crawl cadence vs freshness vs budget is the open trade this decision closes; **O-5** — a standing corpus is needed for LightGBM training snapshots; gate **G2** (Meetup quota scoping) remains a design-blocking spike this topology must survive either way.

## Options considered

1. **Query-time federation minimalist.** Keep the durable store as thin as the quota forces — a slim tenant-neutral identity registry, a Ticketmaster-only catalog with a 14-day horizon, and short-TTL per-user working sets — and federate everything else live at request time under the user's own credential through a FederationGateway with stale-while-revalidate. Rejected as base because the 14-day horizon breaks NFR-1 outright: an event published for >14 days out is not discoverable within 6h — it is not discoverable at all until T-14d — and the option's headroom and corpus numbers are artifacts of that truncated horizon. It further places SerpApi's 1–3s+ latency variance and synchronous fuzzy identity resolution on the interactive hot path, where a mis-merge under latency pressure keys the wrong calendar hash (FR-9.2); its shared identity registry holds match-key metadata of private per-user Meetup events behind a single visibility predicate — the weakest NFR-7 posture offered; its own G2 contingency concedes it grows back the Meetup crawl it rejected; and with no standing corpus it starves the LightGBM training snapshots O-5 depends on.

2. **Four-schema staged pipeline.** Explicit raw → versioned staging → versioned canonical → rebuildable serving schemas with per-stage watermark workers, so the whole catalog is a deterministic function of retained raw payloads. Rejected as base — while its replay/versioning payoff is real and is grafted into the decision — because the full machinery (per-stage workers, a separately rebuildable serving projection, 2–3× storage) is over-tooled at 150–500k rows: the identical guarantees (catalog as a deterministic function of raw, quota-free re-normalize and shadow re-merge, zero-model-call rebuild) are achieved with raw retention, version-stamp columns, and persisted embeddings inside two logical schema families. Its 20-metro launch anchor is grounded in no signed decision, and its newest-sorted delta crawl as the sole NFR-1 mechanism leaves far-out modification detection to a daily backfill on unverified TM sort semantics; it is kept only as a post-launch budget-optimization spike.

3. **Streaming ingestion (Kafka/CDC) and/or any second search store (OpenSearch, managed vector DB).** Rejected: no binding freshness target is tighter than 6h, so idempotent batch micro-cycles via Temporal Schedules meet NFR-1 with ≤15 min pipeline lag and are replay-safe by upsert keying; the evidence base explicitly recommends against a second search cluster at this corpus, and a sync pipeline between stores is the classic origin of exactly the AC-17/AC-21 consistency violations this decision is judged on. The transactional outbox already required by FR-8.9 covers the only streaming-shaped need — change signals to workflows.

4. **Single-Postgres catalog, hardened with option 2's provenance mechanisms (chosen).** One cluster as store of record and serving index, batch micro-cycle ingest, ingest-time dedup for the shared catalog and query-time dedup only for the small per-user overlay through one pinned match library, plus raw retention, version stamps, shadow re-merge, and an append-only alias chain. One rider trimmed at adoption: the promotion of per-user-token-fetched "verifiably public" Meetup events into the shared catalog is rejected at launch, because it makes a single classification predicate the only gate against an NFR-7 tenant-isolation incident and silently answers an unexamined compliance question — whether Meetup's ToS permits redistributing data fetched under user A's OAuth grant to all tenants. The SerpApi funnel already lands public Meetup events in the catalog through a shared, tenant-neutral key.

## Decision

We will run one Postgres 16 cluster (managed Multi-AZ primary + 1 read replica, PITR/WAL, pgBouncer transaction mode with SET LOCAL) as both store of record and serving index, holding two logical schema families. The CATALOG family is tenant-neutral — no tenant_id column exists anywhere in it and no credentialed writer touches it: `raw_source_events` (append-only JSONB, UNIQUE(source, source_event_id, content_hash), monthly partitions, detached to S3 parquet after 90 days; an identical-hash re-crawl is a no-op), `canonical_events` (UUID minted exactly once; schema.org/Event columns including eventStatus and IANA tz; pgvector HNSW on the embedding; a GENERATED tsvector column with GIN; pg_trgm GIN on normalized title; btree (city_norm, start_date) for dedup blocking; normalizer_version + merge_version stamps), `event_source_links` (AC-17 all-URLs retention), an append-only `canonical_alias` chain, a `sources` policy table (FR-10.1), the TM budget ledger, and the transactional outbox. The TENANT family is RLS-forced (non-owner, non-BYPASSRLS role, tenant_id leading-indexed per FR-1.3) and holds overlay rows, preferences, and lifecycle.

We will ingest by batch micro-cycles on Temporal Schedules — no Kafka, no second datastore. The TM catalog crawler is one of exactly two code paths linked against the TM client (the other is the reconcile re-poller), so AC-21's zero-added-tenant-calls holds by construction: every 5h30m it enumerates cells = 25 metros × 5 segments × {0–14d, 15–90d} windows; a BudgetLedger decrement strictly precedes every dispatch (soft cap 4,500/day, 500 reserved) behind a global 2 rps token bucket; the adapter makes size×page-violating requests unconstructible (size=200, pages 0–4); cells projected >1,000 items split by genre then date-bisection, never deep-page. Typical spend ≈ 2,000 calls/day (~40% of budget); 5h30m + ≤15 min pipeline lag holds publication→discoverable ≤6h. A by-id reconcile re-poller covers every registered/scheduled TM event every 6h from the 500-call reserve, so NFR-17 never depends on cell cadence. The SerpApi sweeper runs 4 templates × 25 metros × 4 cycles/day = 400 searches/day (~12k/mo on the $150/15k plan). Enrichment is dedup-gated (FR-3.6): every funnel candidate runs the canonical match first; a match only appends its registration URL and dispatches no fetch.

We will dedup at ingest for the shared catalog and at query time only for the per-user overlay, both through one pinned match-function library (blocking on (city_norm, start_date ±1d) under an advisory lock; pg_trgm/Jaro-Winkler title + embedding cosine + time delta + geo delta), verified by the AC-17 CI fixture (same event via TM + Meetup + SerpApi ⇒ one merged record, three URLs). Identity discipline: canonical_event_id is minted once and never reused; identity repair only APPENDs canonical_alias rows (loser→survivor); every consumer — the FR-8.1 workflowId, the FR-9.2 calendar key — resolves through one alias-resolver library; split tooling ships at launch. Every raw row carries content_hash and every derived row carries normalizer_version/merge_version, so dedup recalibration and source-page drift are absorbed by shadow re-merge over retained raw behind a merge/split-count diff gate — at zero TM quota cost, the one budget that can never fund a re-fetch.

We will run per-user Meetup/Luma discovery inside the EventRequest workflow under the requesting user's own credential only (adapter constructors require a tenant-bound credential handle — FR-3.5/AC-24), on a 2s budget with stale-while-revalidate (working-set freshness stamp 30–60 min, retention 24h). All per-user results stay inside the tenant RLS boundary; no promotion to the shared catalog exists at launch, and the adversarial two-tenant suite gains a member-gated-event fixture asserting zero reachability from tenant B including via the retrieval union. Serving is one SQL query on the read replica under SET LOCAL: (catalog serving rows UNION ALL tenant working set under forced RLS) → pgvector ANN top-k + BM25 top-k with filters pushed down → RRF k≈60 → 100–200 candidates deduped on canonical_event_id → cross-encoder → LightGBM → optional top-10 LLM tie-break; p95 ≈2.5s against the 5s NFR-1 budget, with zero live TM or SerpApi calls on the path. Under budget pressure (80% ledger) we degrade the 15–90d window first to 12h (~81-metro ceiling) then 24h (~105), never the 0–14d window. Policy flags are data rows read behind a ≤30s LISTEN/NOTIFY-invalidated cache consulted by both discovery dispatch and the FR-5.9 register check (AC-23); crawler hash-deltas on active-lifecycle events write outbox rows in the same transaction, relayed as Temporal signals (FR-8.7/8.9).

## Consequences

**Easier:** one backup story, one query surface, one failure domain to operate; AC-17/AC-21 hold by construction rather than by cross-store sync discipline; dedup recalibration, normalizer fixes, and source-drift absorption cost replay, not quota; the standing corpus feeds LightGBM training snapshots (O-5); rows are BM25-discoverable immediately via the generated tsvector and degrade to single-leg retrieval — never invisibility — when embeddings lag; NFR-17 detection is decoupled from crawl cadence by the reserve-funded re-poller; tenant isolation needs no classification judgment because no promotion path exists.

**Harder / risks accepted:** single-cluster blast radius — a pathological ingest event (vacuum storm, HNSW bloat, migration lock) shares a failure domain with serving; accepted deliberately, mitigated by replica-pinned serving, throttleable batch ingest (ingest waits, the user request does not), and minutes-scale reindex at this corpus size. Meetup/Luma freshness is one working-set TTL old between requests, and Meetup-exclusive events reach other tenants only via the SerpApi funnel — a cross-tenant catalog benefit deliberately sacrificed. SerpApi is a single third-party proxy carrying all Eventbrite and (practically) Luma discovery; an outage bounds their staleness to its length. Dedup threshold calibration remains medium-confidence: a false merge propagates into calendar keys and workflowIds, mitigated by conservative thresholds, the alias/split machinery, and shadow re-merge. Ultra-dense cells that exceed 1,000 items after all splits truncate at the API cap — a declared limitation no topology fixes. Metro expansion beyond ~105 requires a discretionary, compliance-gated TM quota raise.

**Follow-ups committed:** split tooling and a labeled dedup-calibration corpus ship at launch, not later; a build-time probe against the live Discovery API validates the ~2-pages-per-cell and split-coverage assumptions before the 6h-both-windows promise is confirmed; the G2 Meetup quota-scoping spike stays design-blocking (no Meetup-lane SLA number before it lands, per NFR-2); the TM quota-raise application proceeds early as a parallel track; the 500-call re-poll reserve (~125 concurrent registered TM events at 6h) is re-sized once G1's request-mix measurement lands; the staged-pipeline delta-crawl idea is retained as a post-launch budget-optimization spike.

**Owner-ratification riders:**
- Launch metro grid = 25, plus the expansion ladder (12h far-window ⇒ ~81 metros, 24h ⇒ ~105, beyond that the TM quota raise) as a pre-decided ops posture.
- NFR-1's ≤6h applies to BOTH date windows at launch, and far-out-window freshness is the explicit degrade chip under budget pressure — a product posture, not an ops improvisation.
- Per-user Meetup promotion to the shared catalog is deferred; flipping it post-launch requires BOTH an adversarial visibility-classification fixture AND a Meetup ToS/commercial-use-clause review of redistributing token-fetched data.
- SerpApi launch spend ratified at 400 searches/day ≈ 12k/mo on the $150/15k plan (scales linearly with metros and templates).
- pgvector HNSW must be re-benchmarked before any 10× corpus growth (≥5M rows) is assumed safe on the single-index, single-cluster posture.
