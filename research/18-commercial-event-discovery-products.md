# Commercial Event-Discovery & Social-Planning Products (2026 State)

> This dimension establishes whether the Events Concierge's core loop (NL intent → cross-source discovery → autonomous RSVP → calendar write → reconcile) is contested space or genuinely empty, and what patterns/pricing/traps the incumbents have already proven.

## Landscape inventory

### Horizontal event platforms with personalized discovery

- **Luma (lu.ma)** (active; https://luma.com/discover) — Event hosting + discovery, default for tech/AI scenes: Discover pages by city/category, follow-a-calendar social graph, personalized Home feed, Sunday-night weekly roundup email; one-click RSVP + .ics export for Luma-hosted events only. Free for attendees; hosts pay ticket fees plus a Luma Plus subscription ($59/mo billed annually as of 2026 — single paid tier, plus 5% platform fee on paid tickets for free-plan hosts). ~35% annual user growth, dominant in mid-market/tech events, mostly bootstrapped (~$3M disclosed seed). Relevance: closest overlap with our beachhead user; feed is passive and Luma-inventory-only — no cross-source discovery, no autonomous RSVP, no cross-source reconcile. Follow/weekly-roundup mechanics are patterns to absorb; also our best-effort browser-RSVP target.
- **Meetup (Bending Spoons)** (active; acquired 2024, parent IPO'd July 2026 at ~$18B; https://www.engadget.com/mobile/meetups-new-mobile-app-is-designed-to-make-it-easier-to-meet-people-irl-150000920.html) — Group-based recurring events. Dec 2025 redesign: attendee gender/age breakdowns, richer profiles, Super Organizer badge, unified app + QR check-in early 2026. No shipped AI concierge features. Organizer subscriptions (raised June 2024), free for members; 20% YoY new registrations, Gen Z + young millennials = 40% of actives. Relevance: largest recurring-events inventory and our Tier-1 API auto-RSVP source; expect API/pricing tightening under Bending Spoons; gender/age breakdown is a ranking signal our spec lacks. Even the most AI-forward owner has NOT built agentic discovery.
- **Partiful** (active; https://partiful.com/discover/nyc) — Gen-Z invite/RSVP platform expanding into discovery: editorially curated Explore feed (NYC, SF, LA, Boston, DC), friends-attending signals; launched paid ticketing June 2, 2026. ~500K MAU Q1 2025 (+400% YoY), 5M new users H1 2025, >60% of actives check weekly, Google Best App 2024, TIME100 2025. Relevance: owns the under-30 social graph and is the incumbent most likely to expand toward concierge — but discovery is editorial + own-inventory + friend-graph, not autonomous or cross-source. Handoff-only in our RSVP tiering (no public API).
- **Posh (posh.vip)** (active; https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/23/event-startup-posh-raises-22m-in-to-focus-on-personalisation-and-event-diversification/) — "TikTok for small events": vertical personalized feed of local (nightlife-heavy) events; create + discover + ticket in one app. $37M Series B March 2026 (FirstMark; ~6M users, ~$350M GMV per Fortune) on top of $22M Series A; $66.8M total. Relevance: the most direct "personalized event feed" competitor, freshly capitalized, and explicitly framing itself around agentic plan-making — still recommendation-only inside own inventory: no cross-source action, no calendar reconciliation.
- **Eventbrite (consumer app)** (active, public, turnaround mode; https://www.stocktitan.net/news/EB/get-into-it-eventbrite-s-reimagined-app-makes-discovering-your-next-6e1l068zuzsf.html) — March 2025 redesigned app: personalized Discover feed, "It Lists" curated by tastemakers (60+ lists, 12 markets), friend-sync social layer, TikTok partnership; 2026 roadmap adds AI-powered recommendation feeds. 89M monthly average users, 270M tickets across 4.7M events (2024), stagnant marketplace growth. Relevance: the biggest inventory pool just repositioned around personalized discovery — yet everything is pull-based; handoff-tier for us (closed search API) and its feed doesn't cross sources.
- **Fever (feverup)** (active; https://newsroom.feverup.com/en-US/250714-fever-secures-100m-strengthening-its-position-as-the-leading-independent-live-entertainment-tech-platform/) — Experience marketplace + media in 40+ countries; behavioral/geo/transactional recommendation engine; pivoted hard into Fever Originals (Candlelight, Bridgerton) — owned-IP inventory; acquired DICE June 2025 and Atom Tickets March 2025. $100M+ round June 2025 (L Catterton, Point72) at ~$1.8B; ~$724M est. 2025 revenue; possible IPO 2026-27. Relevance: proves personalization drives conversion at scale — but Fever's answer to discovery economics was to OWN inventory; its feed pushes what Fever profits from. A user-side agent can position as unbiased. (Originals margin uplift figures circulating — "20-30% higher" — are unverified secondary-source numbers.)
- **DICE** (acquired by Fever, June 2025; https://news.pollstar.com/2025/06/05/ticketer-dice-acquired-by-events-discovery-platform-fever/) — Fan-first gig ticketing with strong personalized Discover feed via Spotify/Apple Music library sync; waiting lists, anti-tout. 10M+ MAU, ~$200M raised, 60K integrated events, ticket sales more than doubled since 2023 — still sold rather than standing alone. Relevance: music-sync taste profiling is the proven cold-start UX pattern; also a consolidation datapoint — even 10M-MAU personalized discovery didn't sustain independence.

### Music/vertical discovery utilities

- **Bandsintown** (active; https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bandsintown-concerts/id471394851) — Concert tracker: follow artists or sync streaming, nearby-show alerts, affiliate links to ticketers. Tens of millions of registered users claimed. 2025-26 quietly added an auto-opt-in "AI" setting that drew backlash for opacity. Relevance: alerts-on-taste is a commoditized layer; the opt-in backlash is a trust lesson for our BYO-credentials agentic model.
- **Songkick** (acquired by Suno, Nov 2025 — effectively pivoted to data asset; https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-is-now-the-controller-of-songkick-user-data-and-is-hiring-a-gm-to-wire-it-into-its-ai-music-platform/) — Concert tracking (Spotify-linked follows, alerts). WMG couldn't grow or sell it in 2024; bundled into a $500M Suno settlement for its multi-year behavioral fan data; Reddit user revolt over the transfer. Relevance: (1) free cross-source concert discovery is not a business; (2) the taste graph (listening + attendance intent) is what acquirers pay for — user-owned/portable framing is both moat and trust differentiator.
- **Resident Advisor (RA Guide)** (active; https://ra.co/) — Electronic-music vertical: 2M+ events, personalized discovery via Spotify/Apple Music sync, world-first sub-genre event filters (2025), two-tap Apple Pay ticketing. 6M users, 192 countries, 20+ years the default in its niche. Relevance: vertical depth beats horizontal breadth in niches — ranking needs source-specific taste features; RA is a discovery connector for nightlife personas.
- **TodayTix** (acquired by MARI / Ari Emanuel, Oct 2025; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TodayTix) — Theater ticketing app (Broadway/West End + 7 metros): discounts, lotteries, rush tickets, tiered Rewards; no real AI layer. 5M+ users, 1,300+ arts partners. Relevance: registration is sometimes a time-sensitive competition (lottery/rush) — an agent that acts instantly on drop has concrete value; our spec under-weights speed-to-RSVP for scarce events.

### Manufactured-supply / delegation social products

- **Timeleft** (active; https://timfrin.substack.com/p/inside-timelefts-journey-to-connecting) — Algorithmically matches 6 strangers to a Wednesday dinner via personality quiz — the system decides, user shows up; expanded to bars, women-only dinners, run clubs. Paid per dinner/subscription. ~EUR 18M ARR (founder-reported) within ~20 months, 150K participants/month, 3M+ users, 200+ cities (deliberately scaled DOWN from 320+ for density). Relevance: strongest willingness-to-pay proof in the landscape — consumers pay to DELEGATE the social decision entirely. But it covers only its own manufactured events.
- **Thursday** (active; https://www.thursday.com/) — Singles-only IRL events, app live only on Thursdays: bar nights, run clubs, comedy in 150+ cities via 200+ hosts; branded singles travel 2026. Relevance: evidence of the IRL-connection wave; scarcity/cadence design pattern; supply source at most.
- **Pie** (active; https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/04/andy-dunns-new-app-pie-uses-ai-to-help-you-make-friends/) — Andy Dunn's IRL social app: AI matches people into curated group hangouts; Creator Fund pays hosts $5-10 per RSVP; entering NYC/LA 2026 with Nadya Okamoto as co-founder. $24M total (per Nov 2024 round coverage; Forerunner, Ev Williams); 250K monthly plan viewers, 50K monthly joiners in Chicago/Austin/SF. Relevance: "AI matches you to a plan" is adjacent to our rank step and shows VC appetite — but Pie manufactures its own supply and doesn't act on external platforms.
- **IRL (app)** (dead 2023; https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/26/irl-shut-down-fake-users/) — Event-discovery-turned-messaging unicorn; ~$200M raised at $1.17B; SEC probe found 95% of claimed 20M MAU fake; shut down June 2023. Relevance: cautionary tale — browse-only discovery had weak organic retention. Retention must come from delivered outcomes; attended events per user is the honest north-star.

### Editorial / media discovery

- **DoStuff Network (Do312/DoNYC/DoLA…)** (active; https://dostuffmedia.com/blog/didstuff-2025) — 22 city event guides, editorial curation, apps, Ticket Membership; 15 years old; claims 60M attendees/year driven. Ad/promoter-funded. Relevance: editorial city-guide discovery is mature and commoditized; durable demand for "what's good this week", but curation alone doesn't compound. Our personalized ranked digest can replace this layer.
- **Time Out** (active, public — increasingly a Markets/hospitality business; https://www.timeout.com/) — Editorial "best things to do" guides; no personalization/RSVP/calendar. Media arm structurally declining (Media -19% vs Market +12% in H1 FY25); profits from physical food-hall Markets. Relevance: confirms discovery media monetizes poorly — the brand pivoted to owning venues. Lists are scrapable inputs, not competition.

### Big-tech and adjacent-category signals

- **Airbnb Experiences (2025 relaunch)** (active; https://skift.com/2025/05/13/airbnb-relaunches-experiences-adds-hotel-style-services-latest-move-to-go-beyond-rentals/) — May 2025 relaunch with AI-personalized app (predictive suggestions by trip/companions/preferences); 2026 added social features; ~$250M invested. Relevance: closest big-tech analog to our loop — but traveler-context, own-marketplace-only, user-initiated. Watch for extension to locals; reinforces urgency of owning the cross-source local-events niche first.
- **Founder Events** (active, tiny; https://apps.apple.com/us/app/founder-events/id6755369462) — Indie iOS app: AI-curated feed of tech/startup events + calendar assistant. No visible traction. Relevance: existence proof that indies are attacking exactly our beachhead persona with AI curation — discovery-only, no RSVP agency; low bar to out-execute; naming-collision check.
- **Qloo** (active, infrastructure; https://www.qloo.com/) — "Taste AI" API: cultural knowledge graph linking preferences across music/dining/travel/entertainment; marketing itself as the preference layer for AI agents. B2B licensing, a16z-backed. Relevance: build-vs-buy option for cold-start taste profiling (music→event genre inference); signals the market expects agents to consume a taste layer.
- **Zest** (active, adjacent; https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/10/zest-launches-a-restaurant-discovery-app-powered-by-where-people-actually-eat/) — June 2026 restaurant-discovery app grounding recommendations in transaction data (where people actually go), not reviews. $1.8M pre-seed (776, Kindred). Relevance: fresh-2026 pattern — ground taste in ACTUAL BEHAVIOR. Our reconcile loop uniquely generates attendance ground truth, a compounding ranking advantage browse-only incumbents can't get.
- **Blockit** (active, adjacent; https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/22/former-sequoia-partners-new-startup-uses-ai-to-negotiate-your-calendar-for-you/) — AI agents autonomously negotiating meeting times agent-to-agent (ex-Sequoia partner, Jan 2026; $5M seed, ~$1,000/yr individual, 200+ companies). Relevance: comfort with an agent autonomously committing time on your calendar is being normalized in 2026 — reduces behavioral-adoption risk of our auto-RSVP + calendar-write step.
- **Grip / Brella / EventPilot (B2B conference AI)** (active, B2B, out of scope; https://www.bizzabo.com/blog/take-your-time-back-ai-event-discovery-attendee-experience) — AI session/meeting recommendations inside single conferences (Grip: 70M+ networking recs/yr). Relevance: where "AI event discovery" search demand currently lands — the consumer cross-source concierge term is unclaimed; SEO/positioning opportunity.

## Verified findings

1. **The closed loop is empty — but NL discovery is no longer.** [adjusted] As of July 2026 no incumbent closes the full loop: event-discovery products (Eventbrite, Luma, Partiful, Posh, Fever/DICE, Meetup) stop at personalized browse-and-buy inside their own inventory. NL-intent discovery, however, now exists: StubHub (Dec 2025), SeatGeek (Apr 2026), and Ticketmaster (Apr 2026) all launched conversational discovery apps inside ChatGPT, making ChatGPT a de facto cross-provider NL discovery layer — but every one of them redirects out to the provider's checkout. None performs autonomous RSVP/purchase on the user's behalf, and none does cross-source calendar reconciliation after registration (within-inventory platforms like Luma do update calendar invites on organizer changes, but only for their own events). The empty space is the autonomous-action + cross-source-reconcile legs, not NL discovery itself.

2. **All incumbents monetize the transaction/inventory side; a user-paid agent is the only incentive-clean rank.** [adjusted] Meetup = organizer subscriptions (raised June 2024); Luma = $59/mo Luma Plus host subscription + 5% fee on free-tier paid tickets; Eventbrite/Posh/Partiful/RA/TodayTix = per-ticket fees (often paid by the attendee at checkout, but earned on organizer inventory); Fever went furthest — its Originals pivot gives its recommendation engine a direct incentive to push Fever-produced events (specific margin-uplift figures are unverified), and it consolidated further via Atom Tickets (Mar 2025) and DICE (Jun 2025). Time Out shifted to owned food halls as media declined. A consumer-subscription concierge paid by the attendee remains the only actor whose ranking can be incentive-clean, and can say so.

3. **Consumers pay to delegate social planning.** [adjusted] Timeleft reached ~EUR 18M ARR (founder-reported, not audited) in ~20 months by choosing the event for the user entirely — 150K participants/month in paid dinners across 200+ cities (deliberately scaled down from 320+ for density), 3M+ users. Pie ($24M raised, per Nov 2024 coverage) pays hosts $5-10 per RSVP via a $1M Creator Fund; Thursday sustains ticketed singles events in 150+ cities. All active mid-2026. Strongest evidence for the core bet: value is removing decision fatigue and follow-through friction, supporting consumer-subscription pricing.

4. **The consumer "AI event concierge with agency" category is unclaimed, and the window is closing.** [confirmed] Targeted and adversarial searches surface only B2B conference tools (Grip/Brella/Bizzabo), taste-API infra (Qloo), tiny indie apps (Founder Events), organizer-side RSVP automation (RSVPify/Acara etc.), and adjacent launches (Zest, Blockit). Nothing consumer-facing does cross-source discovery + auto-RSVP with visible traction. Meanwhile funded incumbents converge on the discovery half: Posh $37M Series B (Mar 2026, explicitly framed around solving "what are we doing tonight?" with AI/agentic plans — the closest incumbent to the concierge category), Partiful ticketing (Jun 2, 2026), Eventbrite AI feed. Caveat inherent to a negative claim: a stealth entrant could exist.

5. **The durable asset is the per-user taste/attendance graph, not the discovery app.** [confirmed] 2025-26 consolidation: Suno took Songkick inside a $500M settlement explicitly to control years of listening-plus-concert-intent data (triggering a user revolt); Fever bought DICE despite its 10M MAU; MARI bought TodayTix; Bending Spoons re-monetizes Meetup. Implications: (a) our compounding asset is the taste profile + verified attendance history the reconcile loop uniquely generates; (b) user-owned/portable/private taste data is both trust differentiator and eventual strategic asset.

6. **Free, ad-supported, cross-source discovery has repeatedly failed standalone.** (high confidence, non-load-bearing) IRL raised ~$200M on 95%-bot MAU; Songkick spent a decade flat under WMG; Time Out/DoStuff survive on ads/venues at small scale. Retention must anchor in delivered outcomes (events attended per user/month) — the north-star and the anti-IRL honesty test.

7. **Music-library sync is the established cold-start taste pattern; vertical ontologies beat flat categories.** (high confidence, non-load-bearing) DICE, RA, Songkick, Bandsintown all bootstrap taste from streaming follows; RA shipped sub-genre event filtering in 2025. Ranking spec should ingest streaming libraries (and optionally calendar history) at onboarding and keep source-specific taste features.

8. **Trust-and-safety signals are becoming table stakes for the "should I go?" decision.** (high confidence, non-load-bearing) Meetup's Dec 2025 redesign made attendee gender/age composition a first-class event-page feature, explicitly to help women assess unfamiliar events; Partiful/Posh lead with friends-attending. Our ranker scores taste/logistics fit but has no requirement for social-composition or crowd signals in the recommendation rationale — a gap when asking a user to trust an auto-RSVP.

9. **Consumer tolerance for agentic features is conditional on explicit consent.** (medium confidence, non-load-bearing) Bandsintown's silent auto-opt-in "AI" toggle drew backlash; Songkick users revolted over data transfer to Suno. For a product holding credentials and acting autonomously, consent UX (per-source autonomy toggles, action logs, undo/un-RSVP) is the adoption gate, not polish.

## Product implications

- **Positioning**: Claim the empty quadrant — "the only service that searches every platform and actually gets you in." NL discovery alone is NO LONGER differentiating (StubHub/SeatGeek/Ticketmaster in ChatGPT); the identity must be autonomous action + cross-source aggregation + reconcile. "AI event concierge" has no consumer owner as of July 2026 — take the category name, but hurry: Posh is explicitly circling it.
- **Wedge user**: urban tech/startup professional living across Luma + Meetup + Partiful + Eventbrite in one city (NYC/SF first) — worst fragmentation pain, densest free-RSVP inventory, matches our auto-RSVP feasibility tiers. Do not launch music/nightlife-first (DICE/RA/Posh own it; paid-ticket-heavy, outside free-RSVP scope).
- **Pricing**: consumer subscription (freemium: digest free, autonomy paid ~$10-20/mo), justified by Timeleft's founder-reported EUR 18M ARR delegation proof. Do NOT monetize via organizer fees or affiliates at launch — user-paid is the neutrality moat vs transaction/inventory-funded incumbent feeds (Fever Originals is the cautionary cite; drop the unverified margin number when citing it).
- **North-star metric**: verified events ATTENDED per user per month — the honest anti-IRL metric and the data flywheel (Zest-style behavior-grounded taste no browse-only incumbent can copy).
- **Missing requirement — taste cold start**: ingest Spotify/Apple Music library (DICE/RA pattern) and optionally past Google Calendar events; evaluate Qloo buy-vs-build; per-vertical taste ontologies (e.g., electronic sub-genres), not flat categories.
- **Missing requirement — social/safety signals in ranking rationale**: surface attendee composition and friends/community signals where available (Meetup gender/age mix; Partiful friends-attending). "Why I picked this" must include who will be there, or auto-RSVP trust stalls — especially for women users.
- **Missing requirement — proactive cadence**: personalized Sunday-evening weekly digest (Luma roundup / It List pattern) with one-tap "book my week"; NL pull alone under-triggers. Add speed-to-register handling for scarce/drop events (TodayTix rush/lottery) as a later paid-tier differentiator.
- **Missing requirement — consent & control UX as a first-class spec section**: per-source autonomy toggles, full action log, one-tap undo/un-RSVP, explicit opt-in for data use; market the taste profile as user-owned and portable (Bandsintown/Songkick backlash lessons).
- **Missing requirement — reconcile scoped correctly**: within-inventory reconcile exists (Luma pushes updated invites for its own events); ship CROSS-SOURCE reconcile (cancel/reschedule detection + auto-un-RSVP across all sources) as the headline demo — it exists nowhere and is instantly legible.
- **Competitive watch / moat**: Partiful (500K MAU +400% YoY, ticketing) and Eventbrite (89M monthly users) will keep improving single-inventory discovery; ChatGPT ticketing apps will keep improving NL discovery. Durable edge: (a) cross-source coverage incumbents are structurally unwilling to build (won't list competitors); (b) acting via the USER'S own accounts (BYO-account agency), which platforms cannot replicate without cannibalizing supply relationships; (c) attendance ground truth from reconcile.
- **GTM**: distribute via the scenes — Luma calendar-follow graph and Meetup groups are public; seed city-by-city (one metro at full depth beats five shallow — Timeleft's deliberate scale-down and Partiful's 5-city Explore rollout).
