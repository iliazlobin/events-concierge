# Open-Source Event Aggregation, Discovery & Calendar Projects

> The OSS record is a 15-year natural experiment on what is buildable, what dies, and what nobody has solved — it tells us what to build ON, what to never build (a platform), and confirms the transactional last mile is untouched.

*Provenance note: the research and fact-check data for this dossier completed inside workflow run `wf_81474205-1bf`, but the dossier file itself was written by the orchestrator after two verify agents wedged on a hung WebFetch (framablog.org). Claim 2's adversarial verification never completed — it is tagged accordingly.*

## Landscape inventory

### Federated / self-hosted event platforms (supply-side, ToS-clean)

- **Mobilizon** (active — handed from Framasoft to Kaihuri 2024, NLnet-funded; https://mobilizon.fr/) — Federated (ActivityPub) event platform: groups publish events, users RSVP on-instance; Elixir/Phoenix; the flagship "de-Facebook events" project. ~79 public instances, 4,188 groups, 376,753 cumulative events (Feb 2025); grant-sustained, not revenue. Relevance: community-posted supply stays thin even after 5 years and real funding — federation solves hosting, not discovery. For us it is free structured SUPPLY: instances expose AP + ICS, worth a cheap connector.
- **Gancio** (active; https://gancio.org/) — Self-hosted "shared agenda for local communities" (Node/Vue, AGPL) with ActivityPub, RSS and ICS export; used by squats/music scenes esp. Italy/Germany. Dozens of live instances (fediverse.observer); HN front page Jul 2023. Relevance: same lesson as Mobilizon; its ICS/AP endpoints are free connector supply.
- **Gathio** (active, pushed Jul 2026; https://github.com/lowercasename/gathio) — Self-destructing, no-registration event pages with lightweight RSVP; ActivityPub. 453 stars. Relevance: demand for frictionless RSVP (no account) — our one-tap handoff should feel this light.
- **radar.squat.net / Demosphere** (active, decade+; https://radar.squat.net/en) — Volunteer-curated activist community calendars with API-ish exports. Relevance: community calendars survive when an ideological community owns them — curation labor is the scarce input, which agents can now substitute.

### Meetup-clone graveyard (platform-side, two-sided cold start)

- **GetTogether** (dead — last push Dec 2023, effectively abandoned ~2020; https://github.com/GetTogetherComm/GetTogether) — Django Meetup.com clone with planned federation. 486 stars, never reached critical mass. Relevance: canonical OSS Meetup-clone death — two-sided cold start killed it, not code quality.
- **freeCodeCamp Chapter** (archived Jun 2023; https://github.com/freeCodeCamp/chapter) — Self-hosted event/meetup tool backed by freeCodeCamp's org and contributor base. 1,921 stars, archived despite a huge sponsor community. Relevance: strongest evidence that even well-resourced orgs fail at platform-side event networks. Do NOT build a platform; build on top of existing supply.
- **OpenMeet** (active; https://github.com/OpenMeet-Team/openmeet-platform) — Current-generation open-source Meetup replacement, ActivityPub ambitions. 59 stars, pushed May 2026. Relevance: the clone attempts keep coming (post-Meetup enshittification), all stuck at the same supply wall — a tailwind for a demand-side concierge instead.
- **open-source-meetup-alternatives (coderbyheart)** (active catalog; https://github.com/coderbyheart/open-source-meetup-alternatives) — Community catalog of ~40 OSS Meetup alternatives with maturity flags; 271 stars; 13+ entries marked inactive (Attendize, Chapter, GetTogether, joind.in, Upcoming, Open Tech Calendar…). Relevance: one-stop mortality table for the platform-clone approach.

### Organizer-side management/ticketing (where OSS actually thrives)

- **pretix** (very active; https://github.com/pretix/pretix) — Production-grade ticket shop for conferences/festivals (Django); plugin ecosystem; hosted SaaS pretix.eu (~2.5% + per-ticket fees). 2,446 stars; the rare sustainable commercial OSS in this space. Relevance: it monetizes ORGANIZERS; its public JSON API/widgets are clean supply for tech events.
- **Hi.Events** (very active, pushed Jul 2026; https://github.com/HiEventsDev/Hi.Events) — Modern open-source ticketing/event management (Laravel/React), AGPL + cloud SaaS. 3,918 stars, fast 2024-2026 growth. Relevance: the fastest-growing OSS in the space is again organizer-side — nobody with traction does consumer discovery.
- **eventyay / Open Event (FOSSASIA)** (active — unified repo; legacy open-event-server archived May 2026; https://github.com/fossasia/eventyay) — Organizer-side management: ticketing, check-in, schedules, badges; runs eventyay.com. 1,640 stars, in development since 2014. Relevance: OSS gravity is on the supply/organizer side, leaving the consumer concierge side empty.
- **Attendize** (dead — unmaintained since 2024; https://github.com/Attendize/Attendize) — Laravel ticketing, formerly the default OSS choice. 4,268 stars yet abandoned; community warns against production use. Relevance: stars are not maintenance; pick connectors by maintenance signal, not popularity.
- **alf.io** (active, pushed Jul 2026; https://github.com/alfio-event/alf.io) — Privacy-focused ticket reservation system (Java). 1,596 stars. Relevance: more organizer-side confirmation; GDPR-first angle resonates in EU.

### Aggregators, scraper fleets & feed plumbing (demand-side, the part that persists)

- **206.events (prestomation/206events)** (active — pushed Jul 10 2026, 889 commits; https://github.com/prestomation/206events) — Seattle events aggregator: scrapes dozens of local sites/ICS/APIs into standardized per-tag ICS calendars + RSS; city-agnostic `city.config.ts`; dedup via `event-duplicate-cache.json` + `event-uncertainty-cache.json`; Google sign-in favorites. 4 stars but heavily maintained — largely BY Claude Code, with a `skills/city-setup` skill and 4 documented agent routines incl. stale-source detection. Relevance: **the single most instructive repo found** — one person + agentic routines now runs a whole-city aggregator with dedup, freshness checks and ICS distribution. Validates our scraper-fleet economics and provides concrete dedup/staleness patterns to copy.
- **City Scrapers (City Bureau)** (active-ish, pushed May 2026; https://github.com/City-Bureau/city-scrapers) — Framework + template to scrape, standardize and share public-meeting events across cities (Scrapy, shared schema, per-city repos). 379 stars, replicated across US cities. Relevance: best prior art for a multi-city scraper FLEET with a shared schema and per-source spider convention; its pain (volunteer parser upkeep) is exactly what agentic maintenance now fixes.
- **facebook-event-scraper (francescov1) + facebook-event-aggregator (Denperidge)** (active May 2026 / semi-active; https://github.com/francescov1/facebook-event-scraper) — Slim FB event scraper (63 stars) + static-site/ICS wrapper (14 stars). Relevance: Facebook events — the biggest dark-supply pool — remain reachable only via fragile ToS-gray scraping; keep FB out of launch scope.
- **MergeCal + ICS-merge long tail (ics-merger, calmerge, camerge, ics-fusion…)** (active, MergeCal pushed Jul 2026; https://github.com/mergecal/mergecal) — Merge/filter multiple iCal feeds into one subscribable feed. 61 stars + ~8 sibling projects; OSS + small paid SaaS. Relevance: ICS is the universal event interchange; a merged-ICS output is a cheap, high-leverage distribution surface (works in Google/Apple/Outlook/Home Assistant with zero API work).
- **Home Assistant ics_calendar (franc6)** (active Jun 2026; https://github.com/franc6/ics_calendar) — Exposes any ICS feed as an HA calendar entity. 200 stars. Relevance: ICS-out gives free smart-home reach; no HA integration does event DISCOVERY.
- **WP Event Aggregator (xylusthemes) / The Events Calendar ecosystem** (active; https://github.com/xylusthemes/wp-event-aggregator) — WordPress plugin importing events from Eventbrite, Meetup, iCal/ICS into WP sites. 20 GH stars but 10k+ wordpress.org active installs; freemium per-connector licenses. Relevance: import connectors are a commodity; the value is not "can pull events" but ranking + acting on them.
- **Apify local-event scraper actors + Browse.ai automations** (active commercial marketplace; https://apify.com/herodcraperdz/local-event-scraper) — Paid maintained scraper actors (local events, AllEvents-by-city, Skool calendars…). Usage-based pricing. Relevance: commodity bootstrap supply — renting maintained scrapers beats writing parser #40 for long-tail sources.

### Auto-RSVP script ecosystem (the demand fossil record)

- **Meetup auto-RSVP script cluster** (2011 → June 2026, continuously re-invented; https://github.com/jbeluch/meetup-rsvper) — jbeluch/meetup-rsvper (2011, API era), jsanchez034 (2013), tpb-dev/meetup-instant-rsvp (2016, 15-min cron to win capacity races), alsugiharto (2019, Selenium — "event is always full after 3 minutes… top 12 players out of 870 members"), siddhantdixit (2023), chidam333 (2023-2025), AnshikaAg03 (Mar 2026), smartclubx/meetup-auto-rsvp (Jun 2026). ~28 independent repos per GitHub search. Relevance: 15 years of continuous re-invention; two durable motives verbatim in READMEs — capacity races and laziness/consistency for trusted groups. See Verified finding 4.

### Standards, datasets & academic

- **OpenActive** (active — UK, W3C community group; https://www.openactive.io/) — UK open-data standard for physical-activity events: schema.org-based opportunity model, RPDE realtime feed sync, and an **Open Booking API**. 50+ UK leisure orgs publish feeds. Relevance: the ONLY serious attempt at a standardized event BOOKING API beyond schema.org/Event; sector- and UK-limited, but RPDE + Open Booking are worth borrowing for connector/reconciliation design.
- **OpenEventDatabase** (niche — 46 stars, commits through Oct 2025; https://github.com/openeventdatabase/backend) — OSM-adjacent open API for geo+time event data. Relevance: the "OpenStreetMap of events" has been tried and never reached critical mass — open comprehensive local-event data does not exist; supply must be built.
- **OpenAgenda (+ Opendatasoft public-events dataset)** (active commercial, France; https://openagenda.com/) — French territorial events platform whose public events are republished as an open dataset/API. Relevance: the only sizable open local-events dataset found — France-centric; useful for EU expansion.
- **Kaggle Event Recommendation Engine Challenge (2013) + Meetup RecSys literature** (historical dataset; https://www.kaggle.com/c/event-recommendation-engine-challenge) — 38k users / 3M events; spawned the event cold-start literature. Relevance: event RecSys's defining problem is COLD-START (every event is new); content+constraint ranking is the right shape; no production OSS recommender to reuse.

- **2025-26 LLM event-finder demo wave** (hackathon-grade; https://github.com/LefterisKyriazanos/EventFinder) — EventFinder (LangGraph+FastAPI), Kairo, EventMatch, FestiFly, find-my-fun, crowd-events-bot… 0-8 stars each; none with users; **none do RSVP, calendar writeback, or reconciliation**. ## Verified findings

1. **[confirmed] No open-source project — out of ~40 surveyed — closes the full concierge loop (discover → rank → RSVP → calendar → reconcile); OSS clusters at the two ends: organizer-side management/ticketing (pretix 2.4k, Hi.Events 3.9k, eventyay 1.6k stars) and feed plumbing (ICS mergers, per-source scrapers).** Adversarial verification (star counts exact via GitHub API; topic sweeps; catalog review; independent counterexample search) found nothing closing the loop. Nuance to carry: standalone Meetup auto-RSVP bots exist as a third micro-cluster — blind, single-platform RSVP automation — but none combines discovery, ranking, calendar, or reconciliation. The owner's 0-star prototype is the most complete open implementation of the loop found (survey judgment).

2. **[unverified — verify agent failed on a hung fetch, twice; researcher confidence: high] Platform-side event networks are a graveyard: supply cold-start, not code, kills them — while demand-side aggregation has no cold-start and survives as long as parsers are maintained.** freeCodeCamp's Chapter archived at 1,921 stars despite a massive sponsor org; GetTogether (486 stars) and Attendize (4,268 stars) abandoned; 13+ of ~40 catalogued Meetup alternatives inactive; Mobilizon "reached maturity" then was handed from Framasoft to nonprofit Kaihuri (2024) on NLnet grants — 376k cumulative events over 5 years across ALL instances is a rounding error vs Meetup/Eventbrite. By contrast, scraper-side aggregators (City Scrapers since 2018, 206.events, radar.squat.net) persist with one committed maintainer because they consume existing supply. The individual data points are directly sourced (repo states verified); only the adversarial pass on the synthesis is missing.

3. **[adjusted] Cross-source dedup, event identity, and freshness remain perpetually re-invented: no shared library, no common cross-source event ID; schema.org/Event is the only widely-adopted schema.** Every aggregator hand-rolls it (206.events' duplicate/uncertainty JSON caches; City Scrapers' per-city schema). Corrections from verification: OpenEventDatabase is low-traction but not dead (commits through Oct 2025); ActivityPub event interop has progressed beyond an ad-hoc thread into **FEP-8a8e**, an NLnet-funded draft standard with a public validator (validate.event-federation.eu) and an Event Bridge 1.0.0 release (Feb 2025) — still draft, not adopted. The only booking-grade standard remains OpenActive (UK, physical-activity only) with RPDE + Open Booking API.

4. **[adjusted] Auto-RSVP is a 15-year-validated user desire (2011–2026) whose history validates our hybrid API+browser architecture — and the demand is alive, not historical.** ~28 independent Meetup auto-RSVP scripts span 2011 to June 2026. Early scripts were API-based; later ones moved to Selenium, driven both by API functional gaps (no RSVP onto waitlisted/capped events — documented as far back as jbeluch's 2011 README) and by Meetup's Aug-2019 API lockdown (open keys killed, OAuth Pro-only) plus the 2021 GraphQL migration. Two durable motives verbatim in READMEs: capacity races ("always full after 3 minutes") and laziness/consistency for trusted groups. Correction of the researcher's framing: the cited repos are dormant but new DIY bots keep appearing every year through mid-2026 — the demand is continuously re-proven and unserved by any maintained product; users keep hand-rolling brittle personal bots.

5. **[unverified — outside the verify budget; researcher confidence: high] Agentic maintenance has flipped the economics of scraper fleets.** 206.events (889 commits, pushed the day of research) runs a whole-city, deduped, ICS-publishing aggregator maintained by documented Claude Code routines including stale-source detection and a city-setup skill for replication. Historically the killer of city aggregators was per-source parser rot ("separate custom parsers for every site, maintained indefinitely"); 206.events externalizes exactly that labor to agent routines; Apify actors offer rented maintenance for long-tail sources. Directly de-risks our connector-fleet plan and provides a city-by-city replication template.

6. **(high confidence) ICS (RFC 5545) is the de-facto universal interchange for events — as both cheap ingest and free distribution — while ActivityPub federation remains niche.** An 8+ project ecosystem exists purely to merge ICS feeds; Home Assistant consumes ICS via a 200-star integration; Gancio/Mobilizon/206.events all export ICS. Publishing each user's "concierge picks" as a private ICS URL would work in every calendar client with zero partnership work.

7. **(medium confidence) Academic event recommendation is cold-start-dominated and offers no production OSS to reuse.** Kaggle 2013 (38k users / 3M events) and the Meetup RecSys literature converge on content/context features (locality, time-of-day, description text, friend attendance). Blesses a content+constraint ranking approach over any "train a recommender" detour at launch scale. (Deep treatment in dossier `22`.)

8. **(medium confidence) Federated platforms (Mobilizon ~79 instances, Gancio, radar.squat.net, Demosphere) constitute a small but free, structured, ToS-clean supply channel with AP/RSS/ICS endpoints.** Modest combined volume, skewed EU/activist/music scenes, but near-zero ingest cost, covering exactly the long-tail community events Eventbrite/Meetup miss. Cheap goodwill + EU coverage differentiation.

## Product implications

- **Position as the demand-side concierge, never a platform.** The OSS record shows platform/network plays die of supply cold-start (Chapter, GetTogether, Attendize, Mobilizon-on-grants) while aggregation-of-existing-supply persists. Our model has no two-sided cold start — "works with the events that already exist, day one, in your city."
- **The wedge is the transactional last mile.** Forty-odd projects and the entire 2025-26 LLM demo wave stop at "here's a list of events"; none RSVP, none write calendars, none reconcile cancellations. Demo scripts should hammer "it actually signs you up and keeps your calendar true," not discovery quality.
- **Auto-RSVP-to-my-trusted-groups is a 15-year, currently unserved demand** — with a second motive to surface as a named feature: **capacity races** ("never miss a spot when a popular event opens"). Add fast-poll/instant-RSVP on watched groups to requirements.
- **Adopt the 206.events operating pattern for supply:** city-agnostic config, per-source connectors, agent-run maintenance routines with stale-source detection, explicit dedup + uncertainty caches. Budget parser upkeep as a recurring agent job (now cheap); rent Apify actors for long-tail sources rather than writing parser #40.
- **Missing requirement exposed: a freshness/staleness subsystem** — per-source last-good-scrape SLAs, stale-source alerts, and event "uncertainty" scoring that downranks unverified listings. No OSS project solved cancel/reschedule reconciliation; ours must, and it should be a headline claim.
- **Invest in cross-source event identity/dedup as a durable asset;** nothing reusable exists. Consider open-sourcing the dedup core or a common event-ID spec later as a credibility/community wedge — the ecosystem visibly wants one (FEP-8a8e momentum).
- **Interchange strategy:** ingest schema.org/Event JSON-LD and ICS feeds FIRST (cheapest, most stable, ToS-clean); scrape only where absent. Ship a per-user private ICS feed of concierge picks as a zero-partnership distribution channel. Add Mobilizon/Gancio/AP connectors for free EU long-tail coverage. Track OpenActive's Open Booking API + RPDE as the only standardized booking rail (UK fitness/activity verticals).
- **Ranking: do not detour into training a recommender** — event RecSys is cold-start-dominated; content+constraint ranking matches the academic consensus. Revisit learned ranking after real attendance feedback accumulates.
- **Competitive watch item (handed to dossier 18's watch-list): Seeker.io** — the closest live product to our discovery half; verify whether it acts (RSVP) or only lists.
